Search strategy

v6.1 removes the big intimidating 40+ search queue. It uses 8 strong searches in the daily route, with quick buttons for days when you only want hotels, tech, local prizes or hidden gems.

  • UK bias: UK city names, UK region parameter, obvious Australia terms excluded and original raffle/paid comping sites excluded from web searches.
  • Travel: hotel, suite, spa, penthouse, retreat, glamping, cabins, boutique hotels, city breaks, cruises, rail-friendly UK cities, Blackpool, London, Edinburgh, Liverpool, York, Leeds, Sheffield, Chester and more.
  • Hidden Gems: anniversary, launch, opening, reopening, milestone, customer appreciation, new menu, rebrand and “to celebrate” giveaways from smaller local businesses.
