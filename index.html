Comp Scout — Giveaway Scanner (v5.3)

Craig-friendly daily search flow: deadlines first, then hotels/stays, holidays/cruises, cash, tech, mattress/home and local prizes. Batch links always appear below in case your browser blocks tabs.

Daily controls

Freshness for new searches controls newly-posted comp searches. Deadline search age controls ending-today/tomorrow/date searches so old 2023/2024 posts are filtered out.

Queue mode opens the next batch only, remembers where you got to and shows clickable backup links. With Max tabs set to 6, press the same button again for the next 6 searches.

Recommended daily sequence

Use these in order. They are deliberately split so you do not drown in tabs.

Search strategy
  • IG Ending Today looks for today's date, words like ends today, last chance, entries close tonight and winner announced tomorrow.
  • IG Ending Tomorrow looks for tomorrow's date, ends tomorrow, winner announced and winner drawn.
  • Hotels & Stays includes London, Edinburgh, Liverpool, York, Leeds, Sheffield, Manchester, Chester, Bath, Oxford, Cambridge, the Lake District, the Peak District and North Wales.
  • Holidays & Cruises covers UK-resident travel prizes, cruises, Jet2/TUI-style holidays, flights and city breaks.
  • Northern Stays prioritises Manchester, Liverpool, York, Leeds, Sheffield, Chester, Harrogate, the Lake District, the Peak District and North Wales.

Deadline searches now add an age filter by default, so ending-today/tomorrow results should mostly come from the past couple of months instead of old competitions. Google can still ignore parts of searches, so treat results as a scout list rather than a perfect filter.
